Data Specialist

Finlync Company Limited
View all jobs
Tsim Sha Tsui, Yau Tsim Mong District (Hybrid)
Business/Systems Analysts (Information & Communication Technology)
Full time
$29,000 – $39,000 per month
Posted 20d ago
Quick apply

Key Responsibilities

1. Risk Analysis

  • Client Trading Behavior Analysis

    • Build multi-dimensional models based on product, region, client segments, and trading strategies

    • Identify high-risk patterns (e.g., martingale, high-frequency scalping, latency arbitrage)

    • Produce regular P&L behavior reports to support risk policy adjustments

  • Client Risk Management

    • Develop and optimize risk algorithms to balance risk and profitability

    • Monitor allocation execution and dynamically adjust client segmentation strategies

    • Collaborate with trading teams to optimize STP/ECN routing

  • Firm-wide Exposure Management

    • Monitor and analyze overall exposure

    • Develop and implement hedging strategies

2. Market Research & Competitor Analysis

  • Track global CFD brokers across:

    • Product offerings (FX, indices, commodities, crypto, etc.)

    • Contract specifications (spreads, leverage, trading hours)

3. Product Design & Implementation

  • Lead new product development:

    • Define parameters (margin, minimum trade size, etc.)

    • Set pricing strategies (spread models, swap calculations)

    • Coordinate with tech teams for platform setup

  • Optimize existing products:

    • Analyze performance data and propose improvements

    • Implement and monitor parameter adjustments

4. Product Operations

  • Maintain products, including:

    • Weekly swap rate updates (Wednesdays)

    • Dividend adjustments (corporate actions)

    • Trading holiday calendar management

5. Data & Reporting

  • Build and maintain databases:

    • Product master data

    • Historical performance

    • Client trading behavior

  • Generate regular reports:

    • Product profitability

    • Client behavior insights

    • New product performance tracking

Requirements

Must-Have

  • Bachelor’s degree or above in Finance, Economics, Mathematics, or related fields

  • 2+ years of experience in CFD product management or data analysis

Skills

  • Advanced Excel; familiar with Power BI/Tableau

  • Proficient in SQL; Python/R is a plus

  • Experience with trading system configuration

Knowledge

  • Strong understanding of CFD products and risk management

  • Familiar with global financial market rules

Preferred

  • Experience working with liquidity providers (LPs)

  • Knowledge of FIX protocol and bridging technology

  • CFA, FRM, or related certifications preferred
